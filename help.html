Frequently Asked Questions

The Admin Dashboard is accessible only to authorized personnel of SDO Navotas with administrator privileges. Regular users will be denied access for security purposes.

Your data is protected in compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012. Only authorized staff can access your information, and all records are securely stored in the system.

If you experience any technical problems, please contact our technical support team through the Help Desk or email address provided in the system. Screenshots and detailed descriptions of the issue will help us assist you faster.

To register, click on "Sign Up" on the navigation menu and fill in your details including your name, email address, and password. After registering, you can log in using your email and password. Make sure to use a valid email address as it will be used for appointment notifications.

After logging in, go to "My Appointments" to view all your appointments and their current status. Statuses include: Pending (awaiting confirmation), Confirmed (approved), Completed (done), or Cancelled.

The system works on any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). You need a stable internet connection, a valid email address, and a device (computer, tablet, or smartphone). JavaScript must be enabled in your browser.

Depending on the purpose of your appointment, you may need: a valid ID, school ID (for students), PSA birth certificate, and any relevant documents related to your request. The specific requirements will be displayed when you create an appointment based on the purpose selected.

Appointment confirmation typically takes 1-2 business days. You will receive a notification via email and can also check the status in your "My Appointments" dashboard. For urgent matters, please contact the office directly.
