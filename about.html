About SDO Navotas

Schools Division Office of Navotas City

Our Mission

The Schools Division Office (SDO) of Navotas City is committed to providing quality, accessible, and equitable education for all learners. We strive to create an environment that nurtures holistic development, academic excellence, and values formation aligned with the Department of Education’s mission of serving the Filipino learner.

Our Vision

We envision a learner-centered and inclusive educational system in Navotas that equips every student with the knowledge, skills, and values necessary to succeed in a dynamic, ever-changing society, while fostering a strong sense of responsibility, resilience, and nationhood.

Our Mandate

The SDO Navotas operates under the Department of Education (DepEd) and is mandated to implement policies, standards, and programs that uphold the goals of basic education. The office ensures that all public and private schools within Navotas comply with educational standards, curriculum requirements, and administrative policies to guarantee the delivery of quality education for every learner.

What We Do

  • 📌 Oversee the administration of public and private schools in Navotas.
  • 📌 Provide training and professional development for teachers and school heads.
  • 📌 Implement and monitor educational programs and initiatives.
  • 📌 Ensure compliance with DepEd standards and policies.
  • 📌 Serve as a link between the community, local government, and the Department of Education.
