About SDO Navotas

Schools Division Office of Navotas City

Our Mission

Developed with the Grade 12 - JOBS ICT strand of Navotas National High School, the Online Appointment System aims to support teachers and the Schools Division Office of Navotas in their goals to enhance efficiency and accessibility. This system desires to address the drawbacks with manual scheduling through developing a digital platform through which appointments can be created, managed, and monitored easily with much accuracy.

Informally, these are the elements of the Online Appointment System- which created by the Grade 12 - JOBS ICT strand students of Navotas National High School with the vision of creating a support system for the teachers and Schools Division Office of Navotas to improve on what can be done in terms of access and efficiency. It tries to help the many problems of manual booking by providing a "live" platform capable of enabling appointment creation, adjustment, and monitoring with speed and accuracy.

The system developed is the Online Appointment System which was built by the Grade 12-JOBS ICT strand students of Navotas National High School with a mission of giving support to teachers and Schools Division Office of Navotas in improving efficiency and accessibility. The users of the system can modify their schedules, create an appointment, and monitor their activities.

The Online Appointment System is created by the work of Grade 12-JOBS ICT strand students of Navotas National High School with a vision of creating a support system for the teachers and Schools Division Office of Navotas to improve in what they do in terms of access and efficiency. It tries to address many of the troubles found with manual booking by providing a "live" platform capable of enabling appointment creation, adjustment, and monitoring with speed and accuracy.

Our Vision

Appointment management will be a process that is going to be innovative, transparent, and accessible, making it slip smooth for teachers and the Division Office. It will be able to work efficiently as it calls for the learning institution to embrace the technology brought by this whole project into a culture of efficiency, accountability, and service to the teachers and administrators in the Navotas education community.

Our Mandate

As a project that embodies the academic part and practical solutions, the Online Appointment System will serve the following purposes:

To enable a working-friendly and reliable platform for teachers' online appointments.

For smooth recording and retrieval of appointment data by the Division Office.

In help of continuous improvement of school operations by introducing advanced digital solutions.

Project Statement

The students of 12th Grade Senior High School-JOBS ICT were proposing you their project entitled the "Online Appointment System." This will help the teachers with all other Schools Division office of Navotas to have a better working environment. It will make appointment-making easy and improves the management of time as well. This facility will make it possible for the staff to arrange the appointments accurately, saving time and being transparent without much human effort compared to traditional manual scheduling.

What We Do

  • 📌 Provide teachers with an accessible online platform to schedule appointments efficiently.
  • 📌 Support the Schools Division Office of Navotas in managing and monitoring appointment records with accuracy.
  • 📌 Reduce manual scheduling tasks by streamlining the appointment process through digital solutions.
  • 📌 Promote transparency and accountability in appointment setting for both teachers and administrators.
  • 📌 Contribute to the improvement of school operations by integrating technology into administrative processes.
