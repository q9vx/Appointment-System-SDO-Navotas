Privacy Policy

Schools Division Office of Navotas City

Commitment to Privacy

The Schools Division Office of Navotas (SDO Navotas) values your right to privacy and is committed to protecting personal data in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

Information We Collect

We collect and process only the information necessary for the purpose of providing efficient appointment scheduling and administrative services. This may include:

  • Personal Information (e.g., full name, contact number, email address)
  • Appointment details (e.g., purpose, preferred date and time)
  • System-generated data (e.g., account credentials, timestamps, and logs)

Purpose of Collection

Your personal information will only be used for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate appointment scheduling and confirmation.
  • To communicate important updates regarding your appointments.
  • To maintain official records for reporting and administrative purposes.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

Data Protection Measures

SDO Navotas implements appropriate organizational, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. Access is strictly limited to authorized personnel only.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes. Information may only be shared under the following circumstances:

  • With your consent.
  • When required by law or competent authority.
  • With authorized government entities in relation to official duties.

Your Rights as a Data Subject

In accordance with the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your data is collected, processed, and used.
  • Access your personal information upon request.
  • Rectify or update inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Withdraw consent to data processing (subject to applicable conditions).
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) in case of violations.

Retention and Disposal

Personal data will be retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill its purpose or as required by law. Once the retention period expires, information shall be securely disposed of in accordance with government standards.

Contact Information

For privacy-related concerns, you may contact the SDO Navotas Data Protection Officer (DPO) through official channels. Specific details (e.g., email, hotline) shall be posted in official communications.
